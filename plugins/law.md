# Plugin law - France and Europe (2026-10-26)

Read by the engine (SKILL.md). Four fixed sections. Sources probed for real on our benches on 2026-10-22 (endpoints, options with key, coded rules, observed discrepancies) and an earlier prototype whose endpoints it takes over; bias catalogue: discipline file `law` (7 defects). "Not established" = not established anywhere. Every key is optional: a source with key and no credential makes zero request and returns `not_configured` / `credential_missing`, never "nothing found"; a route made only of key sources is refused. US law (CourtListener) is set aside (a possible future US plugin).

## Sources

Block read by `tools/gateway.py` (format README.md); wired on 2026-10-28: `eurlex`, `justice-administrative`, `hal`, `theses-fr`, `hudoc`, `hudoc-feed`, `europarl`, `isidore` (keyless), `openalex`, and with the PISTE pair `judilibre` and `legifrance` (v0.4.7, not yet verified on the live service: no credentials at release; without the pair: `skipped` / `not_configured`, 0 request); the other engines are listed `not_wired` and refused cleanly. Every wired law engine filters on the provider side to the eve of T and rechecks every record through the gateway barrier (date of the act, decision, defence or deposit < T; forbidden identifiers - DOI, CELEX, ECLI, NNT, HAL id - and titles); a record without a usable date is refused. Ids: `celex:`, `ja:<court>/<file>` (`ecli:` accepted by `read`), `hal:`, `thesesfr:`, `judilibre:<id>`, `legi:LEGIARTI...|LEGITEXT...`, `jorf:JORFTEXT...`; every record carries `tier`, `order` and, for the administrative courts, `coverage_from`. `citing`/`references` are not served for law ids. The Conseil constitutionnel is not declared: not queryable for search (see 9); Legifrance only through the PISTE API (6). Access marked according to `ACCESS-POLICY.md` (`api` level 1; `page` level 2, pacing 1 request / 2 s / host, declarative UA, cache, stop on 429/403; `browser` reserved and unused (a page behind a JS challenge is reported `bot_challenge`, never worked around); `tos_forbidden`, `technical_block` level 3, listed and disabled). Probes dated 2026-10-26 unless stated.

Audit mode (SKILL.md, section "Audit mode") is recommended for this plugin: exhaustive verification of every atomic claim before any binding legal conclusion; set VP_MODE=audit or ~/.config/vp/mode.

Route order: EU texts and rulings, EU preparatory works, ECHR, administrative courts, Cour de cassation (if token), then doctrine (Isidore, HAL, theses.fr, OpenAlex). Every record carries its **tier** (`primary` / `secondary` / `grey`), its **legal order** (`EU`, `COE`, `FR-ADM`, `FR-JUD`) and, where relevant, its coverage start date. Date bound: the question carries a date; the rule `in_force_at_date` (see Verification) replaces the "day before T" filter of the other plugins, and the gateway refuses the source document and anything later than it (test cases only).

1. **EUR-Lex, Cellar SPARQL - legislation** (`eurlex_legislation`, primary, `EU`) - `access: api`
   - Endpoint: `https://publications.europa.eu/webapi/rdf/sparql` (GET, JSON SPARQL).
   - Query: SPARQL template on the title; fields returned: CELEX, entry into force (`in_force_from`), application (`applies_from`), end of validity (`in_force_to`, **inclusive**), "in force" flag, "declares void" links (`declares_void` and `declares_void_by_preliminary_ruling`, both read); `LIMIT/OFFSET` 100, 300 max, 3 pages. Records grouped **by CELEX**; `_RES` and `_INF` records set aside.
   - Measured traps: an `OPTIONAL` on the ELI returned in HTTP 200 the dates of another act (directive 95/46 with the dates of decision 2021/701); `IF(COUNT())` and `SUBSTR(SAMPLE())` give HTTP 500 "VECSL internal error" = `provider_error`, not empty; citation metadata `work_cites_work` empty for the rulings of July-October 2026 (several months' delay).
   - Date bound: through the validity dates (rule). Key: none. Rate: 2 s local; published limit: not established. Probed for real on 2026-10-22: 200. Option: **EUR-Lex SOAP web service** (free registration, daily cap stated at registration, WSDL 200; does not download the documents) `access: api`, not built.
   - **State (2026-10-28): wired** as engine `eurlex` (sections 1 and 2 together; two queries, legislation `0`/`3` and case law `6`, interleaved, then one details query). Search: title words (3 letters or more, at most 6, EN or FR expression) through the Virtuoso free-text index `bif:contains` (a `CONTAINS(LCASE())` scan timed out on the whole of Cellar); `work_date_document` bounded in the query to the eve of T, `LIMIT 100` each, most recent first, total reported `bounded` (no `COUNT`). Details: one property per `UNION` branch (no `OPTIONAL` join, the ELI trap), grouped by CELEX (twins), both "declares void" predicates read from the voiding ruling with its date. Barrier: every `work_date_document` of the CELEX < T. **Validity under the barrier**: when the notice's `cmr:lastModificationDate` is before T, every field is served as recorded; otherwise only dates before T are served, the end of validity only if before T and no voiding ruling is dated on or after T (directive 2006/24: end = entry into force, written in 2014), the current "in force" flag is masked; voiding rulings dated on or after T are never served. `in_force_from` / `applies_from` = earliest / latest `entry-into-force` value (GDPR: 2016-05-24 / 2018-05-25). `read celex:<id>` (or `read ecli:ECLI:EU:...`): the notice plus the start of the official text (20,000 characters) through Cellar content negotiation (XHTML, EN then FR), and for a longer judgment its operative part ("On those grounds, the Court ... hereby rules" / "Par ces motifs") appended after a marked elision; `eur-lex.europa.eu` HTML answers 202 with an empty body to robots (bot challenge): not used. HTTP 5xx, unreadable answer or timeout (90 s; a first free-text query on a common single word can take 60-90 s) = `provider_error`, never empty. Probe 2026-10-28: "general data protection regulation" 20 records; T 2019-06-01: nothing dated on or after T.

3. **HUDOC (ECHR)** (`hudoc`, primary, `COE`) - `access: page` (endpoint not documented by the Court; `/robots.txt` returns the SPA, no robots); on a 403 challenge: stop and receipt `bot_challenge` (no browser route in this wiring)
   - Endpoint: `https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/query/results?query=contentsitename:ECHR AND (languageisocode:"FRE" OR "ENG") AND (doctype HEJUD|HFJUD|HEDEC|HFDEC) AND (<q>)&select=itemid,docname,appno,kpdate,doctype,importance,respondent,conclusion,ecli,...&sort=kpdate Descending&start=&length=50`.
   - State: on 2026-10-22, 403 Cloudflare challenge from this server; **on 2026-10-26, 200 JSON** with a declarative User-Agent on `.../app/query/results?query=contentsitename:ECHR&select=itemid,docname,kpdate&start=0&length=1` (`resultcount` 232,508). Stability of the 200: not established (on 2026-10-28 HUDOC served `curl` but challenged Python's HTTP client from the test server). On a 403/429: receipt `bot_challenge`, no browser route. Without HUDOC, an ECHR question only receives `needs_confirmation` (measured on our benches).
   - **State (2026-10-28): wired** as engine `hudoc` (level 2 `page`: 3 s, declarative UA, cache, stop at once on 403/404/429; never a browser route). Search: the words of the question, each quoted and joined with AND, collection ECHR, languages ENG/FRE (later translations excluded), doctypes HEJUD/HFJUD/HEDEC/HFDEC, `kpdate` bounded in the query to [start, eve of T] (filter verified live 2026-10-28), most recent first. Barrier: `kpdate` < T, no date -> refused; the importance level is masked (revised after delivery); HUDOC exposes no upload date. Id `hudoc:<itemid>` (e.g. `hudoc:001-229320`) with `ecli`, `application_numbers`, `respondent`, `conclusion`, tier `primary`, order `COE`. `read hudoc:<itemid>` (or `read ecli:ECLI:CE:ECHR:...`): the record re-read under the barrier, then the text of the Court (`/app/conversion/docx/html/body`, 20,000 characters, operative part "FOR THESE REASONS, THE COURT" / "PAR CES MOTIFS, LA COUR" appended after a marked elision). An unknown `select` field answers 404. Terms: ECHR "Copyright and disclaimer" (reproduction free of charge for information and education, source acknowledged; no clause on automated or AI use). **Live probe 2026-10-28**: `curl` with the declarative UA got 200 JSON (search, `kpdate` filter, text), but Python's HTTP client (the gateway) got a Cloudflare "Just a moment" 403: the engine returns the receipt `{status: blocked, reason: bot_challenge}` after one request and stops; no workaround (other client, browser, fingerprint) is wired. Until HUDOC answers the gateway, an ECHR question stays `needs_confirmation`.
   - **HUDOC RSS search feed (`hudoc-feed`, wired 2026-10-28)**: the RSS feed of a HUDOC search, a feature documented in the HUDOC manual (Annex 7: "Click on the RSS button to create an RSS feed for the criteria used in your search"), meant for feed readers. Endpoint `https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/app/transform/rss?library=echreng&query=<same query as hudoc>&sort=kpdate Descending&start=0&length=<n>`. **Live probe 2026-10-28**: 200 RSS to Python's HTTP client with the declarative UA (while the JSON back end answered that client with a Cloudflare 403 the same day); the `kpdate` bound of the query is honoured (items dated up to the bound only). Each item: itemid (`guid`), title, application numbers and formation (`description`, e.g. "71671/16;40190/18 - Chamber Judgment"), decision date (`pubDate` = kpdate, 00:00 GMT). Not given: ECLI, respondent, conclusion, language, importance, total count. Same host and pacing as `hudoc` (3 s, stop on 403/404/429); on a 403/429, or an HTML page instead of RSS: receipt `bot_challenge` after one request, no workaround. Barrier: kpdate within [start, eve of T] in the query, pubDate < T rechecked locally, no date -> refused. Items are `hudoc:<itemid>`, read by the `hudoc` source (record and text, which may still answer `bot_challenge`); a claim resting on the feed record alone (title, date, application numbers) is a primary identification, its content needs the text. Route: `hudoc-feed` first for search, `hudoc` for read.
   - Key: none. Rate: 3 s local (>= level-2 pacing); published limit: not established. Offline option if HUDOC stays unstable: **ECHR-OD** (`echr-opendata.eu`, 502 on 2026-10-26; repository `echr-od/ECHR-OD_process` MIT, monthly announced, freshness to check).

## Discipline biases

Shared catalogue: `vp-bias-catalogue/1`, discipline file `law` (`usage = "signal"`, 7 defects, 12 verified references). Law **is not measured** against the catalogue set; its central biases belong to the **mechanical verifier** on a primary base, not to the LLM judge. The four rules below are coded as verifications (`tools/law_checks.py`, see Verification rules) and taken over here as is with their catalogue name; they can only harden a verdict, never raise it; missing data gives `needs_confirmation`, never a pass.
- **`law.not_in_force`** - rule `in_force_at_date`: text in force **and applicable** at the date of the question. Start <= date; end of validity inclusive in Cellar, exclusive in LEGI; 9999-12-31 and 2999-01-01 = no end; application date later -> `needs_confirmation`; "in force" flag contradicting the dates -> unreliable record (`needs_confirmation`). Real examples: directive 95/46 in force until 2018-05-24 inclusive; GDPR entered into force on 2016-05-24, applicable on 2018-05-25; directive 2006/24 void from the outset (end = entry into force). Also applies to the version of the text (`version_pinned` on the CELEX).
- **`law.wrong_jurisdiction`** - rule `jurisdiction_match`: same order, or order contained in that of the question (`FR` contains `FR-ADM`); EU or ECHR source for a French question -> `needs_confirmation` (direct effect, transposition); other French order (judicial for an administrative question) -> `needs_confirmation`; foreign order -> `unsupported`. Doctrine is exempt, a primary source never, even coming from another plugin. Example: CE 473547 for an EU-law question -> `unsupported`.
- **`law.overruled`** - rule `not_overturned`: a court of last resort (`CJ`, `CO`, `CE`, Cour de cassation) passes; otherwise an explicit history is required (`set_aside_by`, `none` = checked, nothing found); without history (administrative tribunal, HUDOC) -> `needs_confirmation`. Example: T-286/09 (Intel) set aside by C-413/14 P -> `unsupported`.
- **`law.doctrine_for_primary`** - rule `source_tier`: a decisive claim has at least one primary source; doctrine alone -> `needs_confirmation` ("find the primary text"); doctrine next to a primary source is not penalised. Example: KlimaSeniorinnen with HAL and OpenAlex only, HUDOC unavailable -> `needs_confirmation`.
- **`law.non_binding`**: non-binding authority presented as binding; obiter dictum taken for the operative part. The opinions of the Advocates General (CELEX `CC`) are excluded from `eurlex_caselaw` for this reason; they are also a leakage risk in evaluation (they contain almost the whole answer before the ruling).
- **`law.fabricated_citation`**: invented or unverifiable citation (catalogue reference: Mata v. Avianca); every reference must resolve (CELEX, ECLI, decision number) in its primary source.
- **`law.misgrounded`**: real source that does not support the proposition; this is the general case of level 1 (`DISTORTED` / `ABSENT`).
- Data traps observed: dates attributed to the wrong act by Cellar (HTTP 200); Cellar twins; invalidation by preliminary ruling under another predicate; false total of the administrative justice; LEGI states `MODIFIE_MORT_NE` / `VIGUEUR_DIFF` (versions never or not yet in force); date and jurisdiction trap in doctrine (Swiss procedure earlier than the ECHR ruling).
- To be completed by the owner.

## Output format

One **card per legal source** cited (text, decision, doctrine), fields in order (common format: the `card.json` schema checked by `tools/validate_card.py`):
1. **Identification**: type (`text` / `decision` / `doctrine`), official title, **CELEX** or **ECLI** or decision number (and application number for the ECHR), court or author of the act, date of the act or decision, **tier** (`primary` / `secondary` / `grey`), **legal order** (`EU`, `COE`, `FR-ADM`, `FR-JUD`), source consulted and its coverage (`coverage_from`).
2. **Validity** (texts): `in_force_from`, `applies_from`, `in_force_to` (inclusive or exclusive depending on the source), "in force" flag, LEGI state if any, version (consolidated CELEX); **date of the question** and result of `in_force_at_date` (`supported` / `needs_confirmation` / `unsupported`).
3. **History** (decisions): court of last resort or not; `set_aside_by` (identifier) or `none` (checked) or `unknown`; result of `not_overturned`.
4. **Claim** (A1, A2...) decomposed: proposition of law, text or decision that grounds it, legal order targeted by the question, date of the question; result of `jurisdiction_match` and `source_tier`.
5. **Citation**: excerpt from the **official text** (never from a `_RES` summary, a press release or the Advocate General's opinion), checked fragments.
6. **Signals**: `law.*` catalogue ids triggered, with the observable trigger (never a verdict).
7. **Verified status**: level-1 verdict per claim; host verdict = the strictest between the judge and the four rules.

At the end of the report: receipt of the sources (key sources `not_configured`, HUDOC `bot_challenge`, OpenAlex quotas), announced coverage (what the system cannot see: Judilibre and Legifrance without the PISTE pair (`skipped`), Conseil constitutionnel for search, ECHR when HUDOC answers 403), exclusions.

## Verification rules

Level 1 (`tools/verifier/ROLE.md`, the level-1 verifier role; fragments checked by `tools/verifier/citation_check.py`), then the host's mechanical checks, **automated by `tools/law_checks.py`** (rules 1 to 9; no model, standard library, offline by default; lab measurement of the first version of these rules: 20 of 20 mutants killed, control survived, 12 real verifications matched the expected answers on 2026-10-22; the tool's own set, `tests/test_law_checks.py`: 44 of 44 mutants caught, control passing; live check on 2026-10-28, 13 of 13 claims on real Cellar and administrative-court records at two question dates as expected). They take the strictest between the judge's verdict and their own result; missing data gives `needs_confirmation`, never a pass. Audit mode (SKILL.md, section "Audit mode") is recommended for this plugin: exhaustive verification of every atomic claim; set VP_MODE=audit or ~/.config/vp/mode.

**Running it** (SKILL.md section 7, after `verifier.py`, before the corrections): `python3 -I tools/law_checks.py <dir> [--as-of YYYY-MM-DD] [--order EU|COE|FR|FR-ADM|FR-JUD]`. Inputs: `card.json` (a legal source is `{"celex"|"ecli"|"ja"|"hal"|"thesesfr"|"judilibre"|"legi"|"jorf": "...", "title"}` or a key `celex:...`; a claim may add `source.keys` for further anchors, `as_of`, `order`, `decisive: false`, and for rules 8-9 `negative`, `window_from`, `receipt`, `engine`, `total`, `total_status`), `verdicts.json`, and the records saved under `sources/<key>/record.json` (written by `verifier.py` when it reads a law id through the gateway; in injected mode the verifier's `sources/<key>/source.txt` holds the gateway JSON and is read as such; otherwise the fields copied into `card.sources`, origin `card`). `--fetch` re-reads through the gateway (date barrier T) the records missing. Date of the question: claim `as_of`, card `as_of`, a `Question date: YYYY-MM-DD` line of `scope.md`, `--as-of`, else T of the journal (origin written in the output); legal order: claim, card `order`, a `Legal order: ...` line of `scope.md`, `--order`, else unknown (`needs_confirmation`). Output `law_checks.json`: per claim the result of each applicable rule with its observable trigger, the catalogue ids triggered, `verifier_status`, `final_status`; lists `to_apply` and `to_flag`. The tool never edits `REPORT.md`, `card.json` nor `verdicts.json`.

**Applying it** (the orchestrator, same single pass as the downstream rule): a rule `unsupported` maps to a level-1 status, and the final status is the stricter of it and the verifier's (FOUND < UNVERIFIED = SOURCE-UNAVAILABLE < DISTORTED < ABSENT = INVALID): rule 7 (reference that does not resolve) and rule 3 (foreign order) -> `ABSENT`; rule 6 (not the official text) -> `SOURCE-UNAVAILABLE`; rules 1, 2, 4, 8, 9 -> `DISTORTED`, the trigger being the discrepancy (e.g. "no longer in force on 2018-05-25 (end 2018-05-24, inclusive)", "set aside ... by 62014CJ0413"). For each id of `to_apply`, write `final_status` and the `discrepancy` of `law_checks.json` into `verdicts.json` (the only status change allowed besides INVALID), then handle it as SKILL.md section 7 says (DISTORTED corrected from the discrepancy and said "corrected, not re-verified"; ABSENT removed). `needs_confirmation` never changes a status: each id of `to_flag` is listed in the methodology appendix with its `to_confirm` triggers ("needs confirmation: applicable only from 2018-05-25"), and the card's **Signals** field carries the catalogue ids.
1. **`in_force_at_date`** (`law.not_in_force`) on texts: `in_force_from`, `applies_from`, `in_force_to` (inclusive, Cellar) or `valid_from`/`valid_until` (exclusive, LEGI; `legi_state` `MODIFIE_MORT_NE` = never in force), flag `in_force`; mandatory cross-check of the flag and the dates as of `--today` (Cellar was wrong once in HTTP 200); a validity masked by the gateway (notice modified on/after T) reads a missing end as "no end before T" and a missing application date as `needs_confirmation`. On decisions: a decision dated after the date of the question is `unsupported`.
2. **`version_pinned`** (`law.not_in_force`): the card cites the CELEX of the version; without version, `needs_confirmation`; a consolidated CELEX (`0...`) needs its `-YYYYMMDD` date, and a version later than the date of the question is `unsupported`.
3. **`jurisdiction_match`** (`law.wrong_jurisdiction`): `order` field of the record (else `jurisdiction`) against the legal order of the question; `also_binding = { FR = [EU, COE], EU = [COE] }`; `skip_tiers = [secondary, grey]`.
4. **`not_overturned`** (`law.overruled`): history `set_aside_by` and `declared_void_by` (both Cellar annulment predicates, records grouped by CELEX by the gateway); any entry -> `unsupported`, except a set-aside dated after the date of the question (still good law then); `final_courts = [CJ, CO, CE, cc]` pass; an empty list served by the gateway or `none` = checked, nothing found before T; no history field -> `needs_confirmation`.
5. **`source_tier`** (`law.doctrine_for_primary`): `min_tier = primary`; below or unknown -> `needs_confirmation`.
6. **Citation** (`official_text`, `law.non_binding`): the source of a fragment is the official text; a fragment taken from a `_RES`/`_INF` record, a press release or an Advocate General's opinion (CELEX `CC`) makes the claim `SOURCE-UNAVAILABLE` for the proof.
7. **Reference that does not resolve** (`resolves`; CELEX, ECLI, number, application): malformed, or answered "not found" by the gateway or the verifier -> `law.fabricated_citation` flagged and claim `ABSENT`; well formed but no saved record -> `needs_confirmation` (or `--fetch`).
8. **Coverage** (`coverage`): a negative claim (`negative: true`, or a "no decision..." sentence) ("no decision") is admissible only within the coverage window of the source (`coverage_from`) and with the receipt of the query by number or exact phrase; outside the window or with HUDOC blocked -> `needs_confirmation`.
9. **False total, null literals** (`bounded_total`): capped total (1,000) or partial page = `bounded`, never `complete`; a claim with `total: true` that presents a bounded count as complete -> `DISTORTED` ("write at least N"), a bare count -> `needs_confirmation`; `"undefined"`/`"null"` values are read as missing (`null_literals`).
10. **Keys**: no PISTE token nor OpenAlex key appears in a card, a receipt or a URL.
11. **Mechanical retraction check (rule shared by all plugins)**: for every retained DOI (doctrine: HAL, Isidore, theses.fr, OpenAlex), the verifier reads Crossref `/works/<DOI>`: `has-update` then `update-to` (`update-type`: `retraction`, `erratum`, `expression_of_concern`), and cross-checks with Retraction Watch (CSV `crossref/retraction-watch-data`); **no model call** (measured on our benches: 30/30 detected). `unknown` stays `unknown`. For primary sources, the counterpart remains `not_overturned` (rule 4). Done by `tools/finish_run.py` (SKILL.md section 8; `retractions.json`), no gateway call.
